# Book 0 — Source Boundary and the Orientation Question

A restructured rewrite of Book 0 of *R Theory — Volume I*, leading with pictures
and intuition, then the formal audit.

## Read it

Open `index.html` in a browser. The seven graphs are live
[Desmos](https://www.desmos.com) calculators (internet required); if Desmos
fails to load, static PNG fallbacks in `graphs/` are shown instead.

## Structure

- **Part I — See it first.** Seven pictures and the intuition each one carries:
  the four primitives, the UNA pair, the FlatWave sign, the carrier ellipse,
  the transfer-amplitude curve, J versus −J, and the mirror pair.
- **Part II — Then earn it.** The formal T0–T3 audit: primitives, seams,
  UNA construction, FlatWave, carrier, transfer map.
- **Part III — The two theorems.** Representation/preference (what is proved)
  and the automorphism obstruction (what is not).
- **Part IV — Close the book.** Minimal extension, the status ledger, and
  closure.

## Status discipline

Every claim in the text carries its status: checked proof, completed symbolic
check, completed numerical check, standard imported theorem, manuscript
assertion, assumption/axiom, or incorrect/incomplete. Nothing is promoted
beyond what the mathematics supports.
